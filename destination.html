The Crescent Shore

Dahican Beach

A seven-kilometer crescent of pure white sand facing the raw power of the Pacific. Dahican is the beating heart of Mati's coastal soul.

Pro-Grade Surfing

Skimboarding Capital

ISLAND HOPPING

Pujada Bay's Islands

Discover a trio of distinct escapes, each offering a unique vibration of tranquility and pristine beauty.

The Emerald Gem

Pujada Island

The largest of the sisters. An uninhabited paradise of limestone cliffs and crystalline clarity, perfect for deep-sea exploration.

The Quiet Escape

Waniban Island

A quaint, pocket-sized escape known for its fine white sand and vibrant coral gardens just steps from the shore.

The Radiant Sandbar

Oak Island

A shifting sandbar that disappears with the tide, offering an ephemeral and intimate connection with the Pacific horizon.
